    Patients and deaths by group

    Each bar splits the patients in a group into alive and died. The death rate is on the right. Click a bar to filter everything by it. Charts follow the filters on the left.

    Death rates for groups with fewer than 10 patients are shown in grey because one patient more or less changes them a lot.
